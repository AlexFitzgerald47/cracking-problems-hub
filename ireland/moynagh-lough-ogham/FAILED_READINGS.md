# Failed / weakened readings

## `PIBAN SNAVQE` = 'leatherworker's awl' via English/Scots *snob/snab* 'cobbler'

**Status: rejected as evidence.**

A 2026 internet proposal links Old Irish `snob` 'bark' to late English/Scots `snob/snab` 'cobbler' and then interprets the inscription as a craft label. The semantic fit to a leather-working tool is attractive, but the historical bridge is unsupported: standard modern etymological sources date the cobbler sense of English *snob* only to the late eighteenth century and call its origin unknown; the Scots evidence is also late. Without an independently demonstrated medieval etymological link, this is classic short-inscription overfitting.

**Reopening condition:** an early medieval or securely reconstructed Celtic/Germanic form independently connecting `snob/snam` bark/skin with a leatherworker/cobbler occupational term.

## `PIBAN SNAVQE` = 'a tube of bark'

**Status: possible but currently weak, not rejected.**

Stifter's proposal requires several simultaneous hypotheses:

1. `PIBAN` is the common noun *pípán* 'small pipe/tube';
2. `SNAVQE` represents hypothetical genitive `*snamchae`;
3. `V` spells lenited `m` /β̃/;
4. `Q` spells `C` after the /k/~/kʷ/ merger;
5. lenition of `ch` is unmarked;
6. the phrase somehow relates to an object that is solid antler, not bark.

The reading remains linguistically ingenious, but the discovery that **Pípán is also a period-correct personal name** materially lowers the uniqueness of assumption (1). It must now compete against an owner/maker/name reading.

## Reverse side 1 `CRRODOS` = direct match to `RODDOS`

**Status: tempting but not a match.**

The ogham corpus contains the personal-name form `RODDOS`, so the reverse family has an external anchor. But `CRRODOS` differs materially: it carries an initial `C`, an extra `R`, and only one `D`. Stifter independently notes that one of the two adjacent `R` signs may be a correction/improvement, which can reduce one discrepancy, but that still does not turn the sequence into `RODDOS` without further special pleading.

**Reopening condition:** direct image/RTI evidence that one `R` is a cancelled correction and one additional disputed sign is better read `D`, or an attested `CRODOS/RRODOS` name-form.
