Eclipses deep enough over Ireland to be plausibly recorded (magnitude >= 0.90): 37
Unequal-hour length at Armagh on those dates:
  shortest 2203 s (37 min, midwinter)   longest 5166 s (86 min, midsummer)
  median   4333 s (72 min)

A single correct hour statement therefore pins Delta-T to an interval
one unequal hour wide: typically +/-2167 s.
The published 1-sigma on Delta-T in this window is 15-50 s (Stephenson et al. 2016).
  -> one statement is about 54x too coarse to be worth anything.

Intersecting N independent correct statements:
   N   expected interval   half-width   vs published sigma(40 s)
    1       4040 s        +/-  2020 s       50.5x worse
    2       2558 s        +/-  1279 s       32.0x worse
    3       1895 s        +/-   947 s       23.7x worse
    5       1260 s        +/-   630 s       15.7x worse
   10        683 s        +/-   342 s        8.5x worse
   20        359 s        +/-   179 s        4.5x worse
   30        245 s        +/-   123 s        3.1x worse
   50        149 s        +/-    74 s        1.9x worse
  100         74 s        +/-    37 s        0.9x worse

To match the published precision (interval <= 80 s, i.e. +/-40 s)
you would need roughly **139** independent correct hour statements.
The entire Irish astronomical corpus, 442-1133, contains of the order
of twenty records, and only some of them state an hour at all.

== the failure mode that actually kills it ==
The arithmetic above assumes every statement is correct *and* that you
know which contact it describes. Neither holds. AU 664 and Bede report
the same eclipse one unequal hour apart because one records onset and
the other maximum (see RESULTS.md section 1). Assign the phase wrongly
for even one record and its interval is disjoint from the rest:
   1 misassigned among 16: intersection is empty in 94% of draws
   2 misassigned among 17: intersection is empty in 99% of draws

VERDICT: experiment 5 is not worth a session as a Delta-T measurement.
An hour statement is a ~3000 s constraint against a 40 s published
error, the corpus is two orders of magnitude too small to close that,
and a single phase misassignment empties the intersection. Use the
hour statements the other way round, as RESULTS.md section 1 does:
fix Delta-T from the published curve and let it tell you which contact
a notice describes. That question the data can answer.
