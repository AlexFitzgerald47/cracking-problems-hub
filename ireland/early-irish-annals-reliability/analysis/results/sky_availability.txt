**A visible umbral lunar eclipse was 4.2x as common as a solar eclipse
  of magnitude 0.50 or more.** That is the asymmetry the recording-rate
  test turns on: the annals had far more opportunity to record lunar
  eclipses than solar ones, so equal counts in the text would mean a
  strong solar bias, not parity.
