# Sources — Ennis ogham amber bead

## Primary / object-level

1. **OG(H)AM, University of Glasgow, Og(h)am of the Month, January 2024 (Nora White).** Direct examination and photographs of British Museum 1888,0719.119 in December 2023. Records the anomalous `<`-shape, possible `DMVA` / `DMLO` core, split stemline, branch geometry and tentative `?DMVA?VA` reading.
   - https://ogham.glasgow.ac.uk/index.php/ogham-of-the-month/

2. **British Museum, 1888,0719.119.** Ennis amber bead / amulet catalogue object.
   - https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/H_1888-0719-119

3. **Proceedings and Papers (1856), Journal of the Kilkenny and South-East of Ireland Archaeological Society, new series 1(1), pp. 147–169, esp. 149–150.** Earliest published description located in this session; gives provenance, amuletic use and woodcut, but explicitly does not attempt an interpretation.
   - https://www.jstor.org/stable/25502504
   - Public-domain scan: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a7/Proceedings_and_Papers_%28IA_jstor-25502504%29.pdf

## Historical readings

4. **R. A. S. Macalister, Corpus Inscriptionum Insularum Celticarum, vol. 1 (1945), no. 53, pp. 57–58.** Gives `ATUCMLU`, while expressly conceding "little justification" for treating the anomalous V-shaped marks as the U-forfid.
   - https://dokumen.pub/corpus-inscriptionum-insularum-celticarum-vol-1-1.html

5. **Helmut Arntz, “Das Ogom.”** Reports Glenfahan `LMCBDV` and Ennis `MTBCML`; notes that reverse Ennis nearly coincides with Glenfahan. Reports Marstrander's *rúnogam* proposal inserting vowels in alphabetic order to yield `LaMoCuBeDVi`, but judges the result interpretable yet unconvincing.
   - https://libarch.nmu.org.ua/bitstream/handle/GenofondUA/15160/8ac43168d66889635ee85be10eff6cfe.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1

6. **Thomas Johnson Westropp, “A Folklore Survey of County Clare” (1911), Folklore 22(1).** The Clare Libraries transcription assigns `L.M.C.B.D.V.` to the Ennis bead and `L.M.C.B.T.M.` to the Fahan comparator. This conflicts with Arntz, Macalister's later corpus, and the modern corpus assignment of Glenfahan=`LMCBDV`; treat the Westropp assignment as a probable object/transcription swap until checked against the printed plate/footnote.
   - https://clarelibraries.ie/localstudies/folklore/a-folklore-survey-of-county-clare-by-thomas-johnson-westropp/charms-amulets-and-magical-sites/

## Corpus controls

7. **OG(H)AM data v1, `og_readings.csv` / `og_words.csv`.** Machine-readable corpus used only as a control for inherited readings and standard ogham lexical/formulaic inventory. The dataset predates the December 2023 Ennis re-examination, so absence of the new reading is expected rather than evidential.
   - https://github.com/ogi-ogham/ogham-datav1

## Source cautions

- Do not treat Westropp's `LMCBDV` assignment to Ennis as an independent witness: the cross-source object labels conflict.
- Do not treat a modern serialized string such as `?DMVA?VA` as proof that the physical inscription is linear; the source itself reports a fork in the stemline.
- The object's chronology is not fixed by the age of amber or by the 1856 folklore of its use.
