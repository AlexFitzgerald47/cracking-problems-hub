# Hill of Tara – Open Archaeological & Historical Questions

## Statement
Clarify the nature, development, and historical role of the Hill of Tara (Teamhair) complex from the Neolithic through the early medieval period. Integrate archaeological evidence with the extensive medieval literary tradition while maintaining strict source criticism.

## Why it belongs on the board
Tara is the most symbolically important landscape in Irish tradition — associated with kingship, assembly, and sovereignty. Decades of archaeology (including the Discovery Programme work) have revealed a complex multi-period site, yet many questions remain about the relationship between the physical monuments, their use, and the later literary picture.

## Known constraints / previous major attempts
- Major surveys and excavations (especially late 20th / early 21st century).
- Rich but late medieval literary material (dindshenchas, sagas, king lists).
- Ongoing debate about the degree to which early medieval and later texts accurately reflect Iron Age or earlier realities.

## Success criteria
Better integration of the archaeological sequence with critically evaluated textual evidence; clearer models of how the site functioned in different periods; and explicit mapping of remaining uncertainties.

## Key sources & starting points
- Archaeological reports from the Discovery Programme and earlier campaigns
- Medieval Irish literature relating to Tara (with careful dating of texts)
- Landscape and comparative studies of royal/assembly sites in Ireland and beyond
