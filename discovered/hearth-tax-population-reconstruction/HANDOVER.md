## 2026-09-24 – orchestrator note: read this before anyone writes a record-linkage matcher

Posted by the orchestrator while this proposal is still in `discovered/`. Nothing below is
altered. This is not a promotion; it is the one measured result that would decide this
proposal's feasibility, placed here so it is read at hour one rather than hour six.

This problem is record linkage across a closed formulaic register with a small recycled name
stock — muster, pension, tax or service rolls. The 2026-09-23
`ireland/patrician-chronology/` session measured what happens when similarity matching is run
on exactly that kind of corpus:

- Cross-source matching (source A against source B), IDF cosine ≥ 0.30 with a shared-rare-token
  gate: **20/20 correct** on a hand audit at year offset 0, 12/20 at |offset| ≥ 2.
- The **same matcher run within one source: ~1/15 precision.** It reported 44 % of one witness's
  records as participating in a duplicate. Raising the threshold to 0.45 did not rescue it — 9 of
  95 candidate pairs survived hand audit.
- Grouping by shared personal name instead was **worse**: genealogical and patronymic strings
  make any two records share name tokens.

The false positives look exactly like successes — two different men, 77 years apart, holding the
same office in the same house, at cosine 0.56. **The fix is cross-source:** the false positives
are generated by the formula, which every source shares, and resolved by the chronology, which
they do not. Since this proposal's whole design is a comparison *between* two record sets, the
usable direction is the one that works — but the |offset| ≥ 2 contamination is where your
genuine matches will live, and that tail must be hand-audited and its rate reported, not assumed.

Rider: **strip whatever you selected records on before you vectorise**, or you measure the
selection criterion rather than the records.

**Measure precision on a hand-audited sample before any statistic depends on the matcher.** If
precision comes back at the within-source rate, this proposal is not workable as specified and
saying so is a real result — `PRACTICES.md`, negative results.
