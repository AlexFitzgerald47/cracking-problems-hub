## Evidence anchors checked
- Geoffrey S. Smith & Brent C. Landau, *The Secret Gospel of Mark* (Yale UP, 2023), including the missing-manuscript and handwriting chapters: https://academic.oup.com/yale-scholarship-online/book/51315
- Thorlac Turville-Petre, *St Erkenwald: A Critical Edition* (2025); a 2025 review notes that the common-authorship question with the Pearl/Gawain poet is explicitly reopened.
- Biblissima / manuscript catalogues for the **Letter of Prester John**, plus later scholarship identifying additional witnesses: https://portail.biblissima.fr/en/ark:/43093/oedata77a8c0757d398367340acdc2a26c6419f0a6c819
- Martin Shedd, **The Historia Augusta before MS Pal. Lat. 899**, *Classical Quarterly* 71 (2021), 402–421.
- Richelle & Burlingame vs Lemaire & Delorme (2023) on **Mesha Stele line 31**, an explicit disagreement over the image evidence.
- Raman/PCA archaeometry on the **Artemidorus Papyrus** and Jonathan Klawans, *Biblical Forgery and Academic Authentication* (Oxford UP, 2026).
- **Forging Antiquity** project, open Version 2 database of fake papyri (17 July 2026): https://www.forgingantiquity.com/forgeries
- Published scientific studies reach opposing conclusions on the **James Ossuary** and **Jehoash Inscription**, making them suitable only for evidence-led replication, not assumption-led verdicts.

Verification note: physical-object cases were selected only where enough photographs/data/literature appear remotely available to make a bounded audit possible. Lost-object cases were downgraded.

## Time-waster warnings
- Do not let a scientific test date the support and silently treat that as dating the inscription/ink.
- Do not train stylometry on temporally or editorially mismatched corpora without matched-subset controls; the Hub has already measured that confound as large.
- Do not use the legal outcome of an antiquities/forgery case as a proxy for scientific authentication.
