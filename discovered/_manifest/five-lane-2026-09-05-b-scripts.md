## Evidence anchors checked
- Brent Davis, *The Undeciphered Aegean Scripts* (Cambridge UP, 2026), especially the Cretan Hieroglyphic syllabotactic chapter and corpus overview: https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/undeciphered-aegean-scripts/
- University of Cologne / Bonmann et al., **Partial Decipherment of the Unknown Kushan Script** (2023): https://uni-koeln.de/en/university/news/news/news-detail/research-group-deciphers-enigmatic-ancient-script and DOI 10.1111/1467-968X.12269.
- University of Vienna, **Thesaurus Inscriptionum Raeticarum** / Camunic material (digital epigraphic resource).
- Unicode 17.0 / current Sidetic encoding documentation plus recent specialist treatment describing the language as still poorly known.
- Current Anthropology (2025), **The Language of Teotihuacan Writing** — a new decipherment/language hypothesis suitable for independent falsification.
- University of Chicago, **Egypt, the Levant, and the Rise of the Alphabet**, preparing a free digital edition/database of early alphabetic material: https://neubauercollegium.uchicago.edu/research/egypt-the-levant-and-the-rise-of-the-alphabet
- Mesha / Northwest Semitic items were kept out of this lane and scored in Lane D as contested readings instead.

Verification note: candidates with very small or uneven corpora were deliberately downgraded. “Undeciphered” never means “therefore solvable”; the target must be a narrower structural or predictive question.

## Time-waster warnings
- Do not confuse an online search interface with bulk/programmatic access; establish export/API terms first.
- Do not infer openness from age. Re-check whether a newer paper has already done the proposed test.
- Do not promote a high-scoring fit without a null/control that matches corpus size, transcription noise and search budget.
