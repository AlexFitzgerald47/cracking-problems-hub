## Investigated and rejected or held
- **French court to Count Lauzun in Ireland, 1690:** **reject as unsolved.** Norbert Biermann, *I Suspect Somewhat of Peculiar in His Way of Ciphering: Six Letters from the French Court to the Count of Lauzun in Ireland from 1690* (2020), https://www.apprendre-en-ligne.net/crypto/bibliotheque/PDF/Biermann_Norbert_Six_Letters_To_Lauzun_1690.pdf, recovers the material with cross-archive copies and code reconstruction. A National Library of Ireland record also indexes originals and deciphered copies: https://sources.nli.ie/ . This would be an especially easy false-positive.
- **Moss Twomey / IRA 51-character cryptogram:** reject as unsolved; Richard Bean's 2019 decipherment is reported by University of Queensland: https://news.uq.edu.au/2019-08-23-uq-data-scientist-cracks-near-century-old-crypto-code .
- **British Library Add MS 18983:** historical catalogue search results mention undeciphered cipher in one description, but another entry mentions a seventeenth-century endorsement decoding cipher. No sufficiently specific Irish-connected, clearly open item isolated; hold.
- **Orkney/Manx ogham with difficult readings:** genuine early Gaelic writing questions, but outside geographic Ireland and not shown to be deliberate encryption. Did not propose in this cipher lane.
- **Existing Collins Papers, Kennedy, VORFYDCGT and Clan-na-Gael:** already in `STATUS.md` or `board/TOP_INTEREST.md`; no duplicate packs.
