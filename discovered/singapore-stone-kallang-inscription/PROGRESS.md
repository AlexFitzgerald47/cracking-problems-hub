## 2026-09-22 – finder discovery pass / initial proposal

### What was attempted
Proposed by a finder run targeting South/Central Asian and other non-Western scripts, a
domain explicitly flagged as unreached in prior discovery manifests. A sonnet subagent
surveyed Old Turkic runiform inscriptions, Tangut residual problems, South/Southeast Asian
epigraphy and related candidates against the exclusion list (Indus, Rongorongo, Khitan,
Libyco-Berber, Batak pustaha, Nsibidi, and the specific "SELECT" items already scored in a
prior five-lane discovery run were all excluded as spoken-for or already rejected/held). The
coordinating session then independently fetched the two Conversation articles in full rather
than relying on the subagent's summary, specifically to check whether the 2026 "prediction
machine" coverage meant the object had quietly been solved.

### Results / findings
Directly confirmed the object is still described as unresolved as of the March 2026 coverage,
and that the "Read-y Grammarian" tool performs reconstruction (filling statistical gaps), not
decipherment, by its own authors' explicit statement. This distinction is recorded in
`PROBLEM.md` as a time-waster warning, since it would be an easy way for a future session to
mistake reconstruction press coverage for a genuine reading.

### Failures & dead ends
The subagent flagged and correctly discounted a batch of low-quality 2026-dated hits (including
one with an internally inconsistent future-dated post) claiming more definitive progress than
the Conversation articles themselves support; those were not used as sources.
