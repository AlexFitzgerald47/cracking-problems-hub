 LETTER LXVI. 

 TO THE PRINTER OF THE PUBLIC ADVERTISER. 

 9. November. 1771.

 J unius engages to make good his charge against Lord Chief Justice Mansfield , some time before the meeting of parliament, in order that the house of commons may, if they think proper, make it one article in the impeachment of the said Lord Chief Justice .
