## 2026-09-17 – Finder discovery pass

### What was attempted
Searched historical cipher inventories, original manuscript calendars and later-solution leads; compared against the hub's active cipher and Irish queues.

### Results / findings
A historical cipher inventory identifies many partially enciphered letters between the Duke of Ormond, Lord Lieutenant of Ireland, and the Earl of Anglesey, Vice-Treasurer, in 1663–64. Its search-index excerpt identifies cipher assignments E=13/14 and THE=246, and flags an undeciphered ciphertext in a calendar volume 5, p. 498. Direct retrieval of that inventory and inspection of the exact calendar page failed in this pass; whether this cited passage belongs to the Ormond–Anglesey system needs verification.

### Failures & dead ends
Direct retrieval of some specialist pages or full editions failed; no ciphertext has been transcribed. This is a candidate for a solution-status audit, **not a claim of a newly proven unsolved cipher**.
