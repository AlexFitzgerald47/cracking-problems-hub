# Harry Fraser residence kill test — first pass

**Date:** 2026-09-05  
**Question:** Does the August 1940 VENONA instruction that BROWN should "move to his old flat" fit Harry Fraser's independently documented residence chronology?

## Prediction

If Harry Fraser was BROWN, then independent 1939–40 address evidence should show at least two plausible residences: a current/new location in summer 1940 and an older London flat to which he could realistically return after the 13 August security scare.

A much stronger result would be a dated move around mid-August 1940. A contradiction — e.g. continuous residence somewhere incompatible with the cable, military absence, or no plausible old London address — would materially weaken the Fraser hypothesis.

## What is independently established

### Pre-Spain / earlier London address

The International Brigade Memorial Trust profile for Henry "Harry" Fraser gives:

- **30 Millfields Road, Hackney, London**

as his address in the International Brigade record set. The profile cites National Archives, International Brigade Archive and RGASPI references.

This is a genuine candidate for the kind of earlier London residence meant by "old flat," but there is no evidence yet that Fraser returned there in August 1940.

### Post-Spain / Ilford residence phase

The IWM-interview-derived Sussex biography states that after returning from Spain and resolving his RAF case:

- Fraser got a job at **Plessey's electronics company in Ilford**;
- while **staying in Ilford**, he met Con Harvey;
- they married in **October 1940**.

Con Harvey was independently an Ilford/Goodmayes CPGB activist. Thus Fraser had demonstrably shifted from an earlier Hackney address into an Ilford phase by the period surrounding 1940.

This is exactly the broad two-location pattern predicted by the BROWN cable, but the date resolution is currently far too weak to count as a match.

## Searches attempted

Targeted searches were run for combinations of:

- Harry / Henry Balfour Fraser + 1939 Register;
- Millfields Road + Fraser + 1940;
- Fraser + Ilford + 1940 address;
- Fraser + Plessey / Cossor + 1940;
- Fraser + Con/Constance Harvey + Oct 1940 marriage;
- Harry Fraser + Goodmayes / Ilford / Hackney.

### Result

No public indexed source located:

- Fraser's exact address on 29 September 1939;
- his exact Ilford address in summer 1940;
- a mid-August 1940 move;
- an electoral-register entry showing a return to 30 Millfields Road;
- a marriage notice giving his October 1940 address.

The repeated search results collapse back to the two already-known facts: **30 Millfields Road before Spain** and **"staying in Ilford" after Spain / around the time he met Con Harvey**.

## Outcome

**Test status: BLOCKED / UNRESOLVED — not passed.**

The residence evidence is intriguing because Fraser has exactly the broad old-London-address → Ilford pattern that makes No. 876 testable. But treating that as confirmation would be confirmation bias. We do not yet know where he lived on 13 August 1940, what "old flat" he could have returned to, or whether he moved at all.

The public-web branch is now exhausted enough that repeating name searches has low expected value.

## Decisive next records

1. **1939 Register entry** for Henry/Harry Balfour Fraser, born 13 Feb 1906.
2. **Hackney electoral register 1938–41** for 30 Millfields Road.
3. **Ilford/Goodmayes electoral registers 1939–41**, ideally cross-searched with Constance Cecilia Harvey and Plessey/Cossor-related addresses.
4. **October 1940 Fraser–Harvey marriage certificate/register entry**, which should give addresses and occupations for both parties.
5. **1976 IWM / Tameside oral-history interview**, which may state where Fraser lodged after Spain and when he moved.
6. **MI5 / International Brigade security records** already cited in the IBMT profile, especially any post-return surveillance or address sheets.

## Falsification rule going forward

Do not promote Fraser above "serious candidate" unless one of the following occurs:

- a dated residence sequence materially matches No. 876; or
- an independent W/T / Soviet / Henry Hughes link supplies equally strong identity evidence.

Conversely, if 1939–40 records show Fraser continuously resident at one address incompatible with the cable's described move, downgrade him sharply.
