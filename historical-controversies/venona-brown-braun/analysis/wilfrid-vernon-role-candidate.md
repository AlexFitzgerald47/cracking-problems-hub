# Role dossier — Wilfrid Foulston Vernon

**Status:** strongest current candidate for **POULTRY-DEALER**; secondary serious candidate for **BROWN/BRAUN**. **Neither identity is established.**  
**Date:** 2026-09-06 continuation pass.

## Why Vernon changes the problem

The previous candidate search had split BROWN into two possible roles:

- an operational/Communist intermediary; and
- a separate Henry Hughes technical source.

Wilfrid Foulston Vernon is the first person found who independently combines:

1. a **proven pre-war Soviet espionage record involving technical documents**;
2. Communist/left organisational activity in London in early 1940;
3. direct, recorded contact with a Soviet Embassy official in January 1940;
4. a July 1940 place inside **Tom Wintringham's Osterley Park Home Guard school**;
5. engineering/technical expertise and handling of military inventions/explosives;
6. multiple London residences in early 1940.

Most importantly, those facts fit **POULTRY-DEALER's own four-message information fingerprint** exceptionally well. Vernon should therefore be tested first as POULTRY-DEALER, not forced into the BROWN role.

## Part I — the primary POULTRY-DEALER fingerprint

VENONA annotators explicitly leave POULTRY-DEALER / KURNIK unidentified and list four London messages.

### P-876 — 13 Aug 1940
POULTRY-DEALER reports that a Communist Party **Area Secretary** knows about a W/T set obtained through/from BROWN. He judges BROWN devoted to the Soviet cause but talkative.

**Information sphere:** Communist Party internal/security gossip + personal knowledge of BROWN.

### P-882 — 14 Aug 1940
The **next day**, POULTRY-DEALER reports on a proposal made to **Tom Wintringham**, former International Brigade battalion commander, and accurately says Wintringham is then working in a special school applying Spanish anti-tank methods to the Home Guard.

**Information sphere:** Wintringham / International Brigade / Osterley Home Guard milieu.

### P-1112 — 4 Oct 1940
POULTRY-DEALER reports:

- Eastern Command Headquarters, formerly at **Hounslow**, has moved partly to Ashford and Ealing because of bombing;
- 70–80 small river-type vessels have arrived at Hull from the USA and are thought intended for use against German invasion barges;
- a Bristol Aircraft Factory raid caused specific workshop/casualty/shelter effects.

**Information sphere:** Home Guard / military disposition / industrial-war information.

### P-1141 — 9 Oct 1940
Repeats/corrects the Eastern Command move information.

**Overall POULTRY prediction:** the person should plausibly sit where Communist political contacts, Wintringham/Osterley, and wartime military/industrial information overlap.

| Personal knowledge of BROWN | No independent identity/contact evidence yet. | **Missing / decisive** |

### Why the Wintringham match is unusually discriminating

POULTRY's 14 Aug report is not merely that Wintringham exists. It gives **his current wartime employment at the new Home Guard school and the Spanish anti-tank character of the work**.

Vernon was physically on that school's staff from July 1940. This is a much tighter contemporary match than candidates whose only connection is CPGB membership or Spanish-Civil-War politics.

### Why the Soviet link is not generic guilt-by-association

The Soviet connection is independently documented and predates the VENONA traffic.

A British intelligence survey written in 1955 (TNA KV 3/417, reproduced/quoted by later researchers) states that Vernon:

- was active in CPGB activities from about 1934;
- visited the USSR in 1935 and 1936;
- from 1936 passed secret information through Weiss, first to HARRY II and later Henri Robinson.

MI5's Vernon files (KV 2/992–996) later produced his admission that he had been recruited through the Meredith/Weiss network and had committed espionage.

This establishes that Vernon had already performed a **technical-document collection/conduit role for Soviet intelligence**, which is structurally similar to the BROWN/POULTRY network under investigation.

Vernon also deserves testing as BROWN, but the fit is currently weaker than POULTRY.

| BROWN constraint | Vernon evidence | Current strength |
|---|---|---|
| Devoted/pro-Soviet | Proven pre-war Soviet espionage + Soviet/Communist political activity. | **Very strong** |

| Technical-document intermediary | Proven at RAE: passed technical information through a Soviet agent chain. | **Very strong role analogue** |
| W/T set / radio logistics | No direct 1940 clandestine-W/T evidence found. STANLEY, not BROWN, is the actual radio technician, reducing the importance of this gap. | **Unknown but no longer fatal** |
| Old flat / residence move | Jan 1940: The Hollies, Holly Park, Crouch End. Mar 1940: Pembroke Villas, Pembroke Square, SW8. This proves address change before the cable but not an Aug return to an old flat. | **Testable, unresolved** |
| Henry Hughes source access | No Vernon ↔ Henry Hughes / Sproule connection found. | **Missing / load-bearing** |
| Current Soviet contact | Jan 1940 Soviet Embassy lunch with Ivan Popov. | **Strong contextual** |

### BROWN interpretation

Vernon's biography makes him a surprisingly good **H2/H3-type BROWN candidate**: a technical, politically committed former Soviet source who could understand and forward industrial information without working at Henry Hughes.

But if Vernon were BROWN, POULTRY-DEALER would have to be someone else who knew both Vernon and Wintringham/Osterley. Since Vernon himself sits directly inside the Wintringham information source, **Vernon = POULTRY currently requires fewer unobserved steps**.
