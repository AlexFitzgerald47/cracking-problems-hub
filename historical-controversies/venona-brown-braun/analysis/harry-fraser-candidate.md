# Candidate dossier — Harry (Henry Balfour) Fraser

**Status:** serious BROWN/BRAUN candidate under the handler / CPGB-collector models; **not identified**.  
**Date:** 2026-09-05 continuation pass.

## Why this candidate emerged

BROWN's three recovered messages force two kinds of constraints at once:

1. **Operational / political:** trusted enough to act as conduit in clandestine W/T arrangements, known within a Communist Party network, described as devoted to the Soviet cause but talkative, and possessing an "old flat" to which he could be sent back.
2. **Technical-source access:** by 4 Sep 1940 he could pass a report whose details came direct from the Henry Hughes production line about a horizontal echo-sounder.

The first pass searched Henry Hughes engineers and found strong technical-source candidates but no one satisfying the operational constraints. Harry Fraser is the first person found who fits several of the operational constraints independently while also sitting in the relevant East London electronics milieu.

## Primary BROWN constraints

### London No. 798 — 22 Jul 1940
- GRU intends to find a flat for BROWN with a W/T set.
- STENLI receives Soviet material via BROWN.
- BROWN is to be put in touch with MINISTR or another Soviet contact.

Source: VENONA London GRU aggregate, 3/PPDT/T30.

### London No. 876 — 13 Aug 1940
- POULTRY-DEALER reports that a Communist Party Area Secretary knows of a W/T set obtained from BROWN.
- POULTRY-DEALER judges BROWN devoted to the Soviet cause but talkative.
- Security response: BROWN is to move to his **old flat**; STANLEY stops radio work for a fortnight and moves area.

Source: VENONA London GRU aggregate, 3/PPDT/T45.

### London No. 976 — 4 Sep 1940
- BROWN signs a report saying details received direct from the production line at Henry Hughes works have compromised a device for determining underwater range between submarines and surface vessels by reflection — a "horizontal echo-sounder."

Source: VENONA London GRU aggregate, 3/PPDT/T50.

## Particularly important network result: POULTRY-DEALER and the International Brigade

This is the strongest new contextual observation of the continuation pass.

The same unidentified POULTRY-DEALER who on **13 Aug** reports the Communist Party leak around BROWN and the W/T set, on **14 Aug** reports a political proposal made to **Tom Wintringham**, explicitly described in the cable as a former International Brigade battalion commander, then working on Spanish anti-tank methods for the Home Guard.

That does **not** identify POULTRY-DEALER as an International Brigader and does not identify Fraser as BROWN. It does show, directly from consecutive primary cables, that the source who knew about BROWN was reporting inside the **CPGB / International Brigade / wartime military-information milieu**. Fraser belongs to that milieu independently.

## The Springhall edge

Fraser's own 1976 IWM-derived biography records that in 1937 he personally went to Dave Springhall after his first attempt to join the International Brigades was rejected; Springhall authorised and facilitated his journey.

This connection matters because Springhall was not merely another Communist acquaintance. Later documentary scholarship shows:

- he became CPGB National Organiser in 1940;
- MI5 files show strong suspicion of Soviet Embassy contact by 1943;
- he was convicted in 1943 after obtaining secret Air Ministry material and was also found to have obtained classified SOE information from Desmond Uren;
- Alexander Foote's published account says Springhall recruited him into Red Army intelligence after Spain.

This upgrades Fraser from "generic Communist engineer" to someone who had a documented personal route to a Party official later involved in Soviet intelligence collection. It still does **not** prove Springhall recruited Fraser or that the relationship persisted into 1940.

## The Beurton edge

Pauline Fraser wrote in the International Brigade Memorial Trust magazine that her father **knew Len Beurton from the British Battalion**.

The UK National Archives catalogue (KV 6/44) independently describes Leon Beurton as:

- British;
- an International Brigader in Spain;
- introduced to Ursula Kuczynski through Alexander Foote;
- active in the Rote Drei Soviet network as a **wireless operator**.

This matters because BROWN's earliest recovered function is clandestine W/T logistics. It demonstrates that Fraser's real Spanish social network contained at least one man who moved directly into Soviet clandestine wireless work. It is **network plausibility**, not evidence that Fraser himself did so. Beurton was in the continental network and is not proposed as the London BROWN.

## Employer chronology correction

Do not repeat the earlier shorthand "Plessey 1939–45" as established fact.

The accessible IWM-derived Fraser biography says:

1. after returning from Spain in Dec 1938 and resolving his RAF desertion, he "landed a job at Plesseys electronics company in Ilford";
2. while staying in Ilford he met Con Harvey; they married Oct 1940;
3. "during the Second World War" he worked on radar under an essential-work order for **Cossor**.

This leaves the exact date of transition Plessey → Cossor unresolved. It is entirely possible Fraser was already at Cossor by summer 1940. Until the IWM/Tameside interview is inspected, treat **East London electronics/radio competence** as established but his precise 4 Sep 1940 employer as unknown.

## Why Fraser is better under H2/H3 than H1

There is currently **no direct evidence Fraser worked for Henry Hughes**. Forcing him into H1 would therefore be special pleading.

He is more interesting under:

- **H2 handler:** a technically literate Communist intermediary capable of understanding/forwarding material received from a Henry Hughes insider;
- **H3 CPGB collector:** a trusted local Communist whose party/International Brigade network could reach skilled industrial workers and whose electronics competence made him useful for W/T logistics.

Under those models, the Henry Hughes insider can remain a separate person — with the Sproule/Hughes directional-acoustics team currently the strongest technical source pool.

## Predictions that can kill or strengthen the hypothesis

### P1 — residence
If Fraser = BROWN, electoral-register / MI5 / personnel evidence should make an Aug 1940 return to an earlier London flat plausible. Search especially 30 Millfields Road, Hackney versus an Ilford address.

**Kill condition:** records show he continuously lived at one fixed address inconsistent with the cable's security move, or was resident elsewhere throughout Jul–Sep 1940.

### P2 — 1940 employer
Resolve exactly when Fraser left Plessey and started Cossor.

**Kill/weakening condition:** an employer/location chronology that makes BROWN's Sep 1940 Henry Hughes channel implausible and provides no local/network bridge.

### P3 — W/T role
The 1976 IWM interview / Tameside Manchester Studies tape may mention radio, amateur wireless, wartime communications, party technical work, residences or contacts.

**Strong update:** independent evidence of wireless-set ownership, radio construction, clandestine communications, or party technical duties in 1939–40.

### P4 — Springhall continuity
Search Springhall's KV files, Fraser's Security Service / International Brigade files and CPGB records for post-Spain contact.

**Strong update:** Fraser named among Springhall's industrial sources, technical contacts, Spanish comrades or communication helpers.

### P5 — Henry Hughes bridge
Search Henry Hughes staff/union/patent/project records for a worker linked to Fraser, Connie Harvey, Ilford CPGB or the International Brigade network.

**Required for a strong identification.** Without this, Fraser remains a network-fit candidate only.
