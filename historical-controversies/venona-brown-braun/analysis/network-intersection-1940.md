## The crucial distinction

The current evidence does **not** produce a closed chain:

`Soviet handler → [identified BROWN] → [identified Henry Hughes source]`

Nor does it yet prove:

`Vernon = POULTRY-DEALER`.

What it does produce is a much more constrained network:

- one side is a real Soviet technical spy physically working with Wintringham in the exact weeks POULTRY reports on him;
- another side is a tightly defined Henry Hughes directional-acoustic project;
- BROWN lies between a Communist operational network and that industrial source.

The next crack should come from finding **one missing observed edge**, not adding more circumstantial traits.

## Highest-value missing edges

Ranked:

1. **Vernon ↔ BROWN** in Jul–Oct 1940 MI5/Soviet records.
2. **Vernon ↔ BARCh/Kremer / Soviet Military Attaché** in 1940.
3. **BROWN candidate ↔ Henry Hughes directional-project worker**.
4. **Named CPGB Area Secretary ↔ BROWN candidate**.
5. **Fraser ↔ Barnard** co-presence/contact at Plessey in early 1940.
6. **Sproule ↔ Soviet/Communist contact** independently documented.

## Search discipline

A new candidate should not be promoted merely for matching ideology + occupation. Promotion now requires at least two **independent network edges**, one of which should touch either:

- Soviet intelligence / Embassy / GRU;
- POULTRY/Wintringham/Area Secretary;
- Henry Hughes/source project.
