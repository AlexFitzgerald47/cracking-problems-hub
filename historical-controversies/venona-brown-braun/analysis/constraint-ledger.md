## Evidence discipline

This file separates **observed cable facts** from **derived hypotheses**. Do not turn a plausible interpretation into a biographical constraint without a source.

## Competing role models

### H1 — BROWN is the Henry Hughes insider
Predictions:
- candidate appears on Henry Hughes payroll / technical staff / production organisation in 1940;
- candidate has direct echo-sounder access;
- independent CP/Soviet or clandestine-radio evidence should exist;
- candidate's residence should plausibly fit BROWN's flat movements.

Failure condition: a candidate has perfect technical access but no plausible way to satisfy C1–C6 after serious archive checking.

### H2 — BROWN is a handler/intermediary for a Henry Hughes insider
Predictions:
- BROWN need not be on Henry Hughes payroll;
- a separate technical person should appear in BROWN's Communist/social/intelligence network;
- cable phrasing “details received direct from the production line” may describe the *source's* proximity, while C1–C6 describe BROWN's operational role.

Failure condition: archival material directly identifies BROWN as an employee/source or shows no separate intermediary stage.

### H3 — BROWN is a CPGB-linked local collector
Predictions:
- strongest biographical traces may sit in CPGB/MI5/local-party files rather than engineering records;
- the relevant Henry Hughes source may be an ordinary worker, draughtsman, tester or shop steward absent from patent literature;
- the “Area Secretary” and BROWN's old flat may be more identifying than the technical report.

Failure condition: BROWN's identity emerges from company/security records with no meaningful CP intermediary role.

## Technical source-pool narrowing

### Donald Orr Sproule
Observed:
- Henry Hughes inventor/engineer, Ilford/London.
- US2350080A priority 26 Nov 1937: compressional-wave indicator; shipboard / echo-sounding relevance.
- GB591533A filed 22 Mar 1939: directional sound-wave apparatus with Arthur Joseph Hughes / Henry Hughes & Son.
- 1998 technical history identifies Sproule as Canadian and says he was later forced from Kelvin Hughes over alleged Russian-spy associations.

Unknown:
- CPGB membership/contact.
- clandestine W/T access.
- 1940 residence.
- whether he was the source of No. 976, BROWN himself, or neither.
- documentary basis for the post-war espionage allegation.

Best falsifier: security/SOE file contents plus 1939 Register/employment/residence records.

### Arthur Joseph Hughes
Observed:
- senior company figure / works-management lineage.
- named on relevant directional-sound patent and echo-depth patents.

Unknown:
- any Soviet/CP connection; none found in first pass.

Use primarily as a **technical-access control**: very high access without current network evidence.

### Albert Beaumont Wood / Frederick Daniel Smith / James Andrew McGeachy
Observed:
- inventors on Henry Hughes-assigned US2076330A for distance measurement by echo reception.

Unknown:
- 1940 Henry Hughes employment/plant access and CP/Soviet connections.

Important: the underlying patent was public years before Sep 1940, so possession of public patent knowledge alone cannot explain a wartime “production line” compromise.

### Philip Francis Everitt
Observed:
- Ilford-based Henry Hughes inventor in the late 1930s, with company instrumentation patents.

Unknown:
- echo-sounder production role; CP/Soviet link; W/T link.

## Highest-information next queries

### Q1 — What exactly is the Sproule “Russian spies” allegation?
Find the documentary chain behind the 1998 history sentence.

Search targets:
- UK National Archives HS 9/1401 and exact Donald Orr Sproule sub-file;
- KV (Security Service) catalogue for SPROULE / Henry Hughes / Kelvin Hughes;
- company histories or Tom Brown / Ian Donald papers that mention Sproule's departure;
- personnel/security-vetting records if catalogued.

Interpretation:
- If allegation predates public VENONA and names Soviet contacts, major update toward Sproule as source/BROWN.
- If allegation is vague, post-hoc, mistaken or derived from the same VENONA cable, downgrade strongly.

### Q2 — Who physically worked on the relevant horizontal/ASDIC production line in summer 1940?
Build roster from:
- Henry Hughes wartime staff/payroll/apprentice records if surviving;
- patent inventors and witnesses;
- Admiralty contracts / production correspondence;
- trade-union/shop-steward records;
- 1939 Register occupations at Hainault/Ilford.

### Q3 — Who was the Communist Party “Area Secretary” in the Henry Hughes/Hainault/Ilford locality in Aug 1940?
This may be more identifying than technical biographies. Once named, enumerate their known contacts and residences from MI5/CPGB records.

### Q4 — Can BROWN's “old flat” be reconstructed?
Look for addresses in:
- MI5 surveillance reports on local CP officials;
- POULTRY-DEALER / STENLI / MINISTR identity files if any become identified;
- Soviet defector / GRU operational records;
- electoral registers / 1939 Register for candidate residences.

### Q5 — Is the Sep 1940 report technically specific enough to match one Henry Hughes project?
Compare cable description against:
- GB591533A directional apparatus;
- US2076330A / GB375375A distance-by-echo apparatus;
- Type 758N and contemporaneous Admiralty ASDIC / horizontal echo-ranging equipment;
- 1940 contract changes that would not have been public in older patents.

A match to a single project can identify its production/test team.

## Candidate scoring rule for next pass

Do not use a single plausibility score. Record each candidate on independent dimensions:

1. 1940 presence/location
2. Henry Hughes technical access
3. exact echo-sounder project access
4. CPGB/local Communist link
5. Soviet/GRU link
6. W/T/radio competence or logistics
7. residence/flat evidence
8. relationship to known BROWN-network actors
9. security-file corroboration
10. contradictions

Require at least one **network** constraint and one **technical/source** constraint before promoting anyone from “lead” to “candidate BROWN.”
