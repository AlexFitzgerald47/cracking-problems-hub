# Candidate dossier — Wilfrid Foulston Vernon as POULTRY-DEALER

**Status:** strongest POULTRY-DEALER candidate found so far; not identified/proven.
**Date:** 2026-09-06.

## Why this matters

POULTRY-DEALER is the source who reports the BROWN/STANLEY security breach on 13 Aug 1940. Identifying POULTRY-DEALER would sharply constrain BROWN through a real contact network.

## Primary VENONA fingerprint of POULTRY-DEALER

The four recovered occurrences are unusually coherent:

1. **13 Aug 1940, London 876** — reports that a Communist Party Area Secretary knows about a W/T set from BROWN; judges BROWN devoted to the Soviet cause but talkative.
2. **14 Aug 1940, London 882** — reports a proposal made to **Tom Wintringham**, former International Brigade battalion commander, then working at the Osterley Park Home Guard school introducing Spanish anti-tank methods.
3. **4 Oct 1940, London 1112** — reports that bombing has caused **Eastern Command HQ at Hounslow** to move partly to Ashford and Ealing; also reports small river-type vessels arriving at Hull and details of the Bristol Aircraft Factory raid.
4. **9 Oct 1940, London 1141** — the Eastern Command/Hounslow report is repeated at Moscow's request.

This predicts someone with (a) Communist-network access, (b) direct/near-direct Wintringham/Osterley access, (c) military/aviation technical knowledge, (d) access to information around Hounslow/west London, and (e) a Soviet intelligence channel.

## Vernon against that fingerprint

### Direct Osterley/Wintringham fit — very strong

Independent history places **Wilfrid Vernon at the unofficial Osterley Park Home Guard school from July 1940**, working directly with Tom Wintringham. He was the explosives expert and constructed the school's first improvised mortar. This is an exceptional fit to POULTRY-DEALER reporting on Wintringham's Home Guard activity on 14 Aug.

### Hounslow geography — very strong

Osterley Park is in the Hounslow/west-London area. POULTRY-DEALER's October report is specifically about Eastern Command HQ at **Hounslow** moving after bombing. A person working at Osterley is geographically and institutionally much better placed than a generic London Communist to hear this.

### Military/aviation technical fit — strong

Vernon had served in the RNAS/RAF, worked in aircraft design, and spent 1925–37 at the Royal Aircraft Establishment. At Osterley he taught/experimented with explosives and improvised weapons. POULTRY-DEALER's October reporting includes aircraft-factory and military observations.

### Communist-network fit — strong

A contemporary security trail places Vernon organizing Communist meetings in Brixton in March 1940. He had longstanding left/Communist associations.

### Actual Soviet-intelligence history — exceptionally strong

Postwar investigation of the Robinson papers identified Vernon as one of Ernest David Weiss's pre-war Soviet/GRU informants. Vernon later admitted under MI5 interrogation that he had engaged in espionage. Secondary work drawing on KV files states he and Frederick Meredith had passed documents to Weiss for transmission to Moscow.

A local-history reconstruction drawing on his MI5 file also records that in **January 1940** MI5 observed Vernon lunching in Kensington with **Ivan Popov, second secretary of the Soviet Embassy**, and obtained a postal-interception warrant.

This is qualitatively different from mere Communist sympathy: Vernon had a demonstrated pre-war Soviet intelligence relationship and a Soviet Embassy contact in the same calendar year as the POULTRY-DEALER cables.

### Address chronology

The same MI5-based account gives Vernon at Crouch End around Jan 1940 and at Pembroke Villas/Pembroke Square in March 1940 while organizing Communist meetings. This establishes London mobility but has not yet been matched to any VENONA residence clue.

## Important caution / potential contradiction

Later MI5 assessments reportedly considered Vernon's known espionage for the Weiss/Robinson network to have ceased before the wartime period (often summarized as no further espionage after 1937). If that assessment is based on affirmative evidence rather than merely the absence of known post-1937 reports, it weighs against POULTRY-DEALER = Vernon.

However, the January 1940 Soviet Embassy lunch and July 1940 Osterley role mean the 1940 hypothesis is directly testable in KV 2/992–996. Do not override the primary VENONA fit with a later summary until the underlying files are checked.

## Why Vernon is probably POULTRY-DEALER rather than BROWN

The 13 Aug cable has POULTRY-DEALER reporting **about BROWN** and assessing BROWN's reliability. Unless the GRU used a bizarre self-reporting construction, the natural reading makes them distinct people.

Thus Vernon, if confirmed as POULTRY-DEALER, becomes a route to BROWN through his 1940 contacts rather than a BROWN candidate himself.

## Predictions / kill tests

1. **KV 2/992–996 contact test:** Vernon surveillance/contact records for Jul–Oct 1940 should show Osterley/Wintringham and perhaps CPGB or Soviet contacts matching the cable dates.
2. **BROWN bridge:** names in Vernon's 1940 contact orbit should be intersected with Fraser, Barnard, Plessey, Henry Hughes/Kelvin Hughes, Sproule, Springhall, and Ilford CPGB.
3. **Hounslow information test:** if Vernon's Osterley/Home Guard role gave him access to Eastern Command movements, this strengthens POULTRY substantially.
4. **Negative:** if KV files establish Vernon had no Soviet/CP intelligence contact in 1940 and was closely monitored in a way incompatible with clandestine reporting, downgrade sharply.
5. **Wording/network:** identify whether the Soviet Embassy official/contact chain around Vernon leads to GRU Military Attaché personnel rather than a separate Soviet service.

## Current assessment

**Vernon is the strongest POULTRY-DEALER candidate found so far.** The convergence is unusually specific: direct Wintringham/Osterley role at the exact time; Hounslow geography; military/aviation expertise; active Communist organizing in 1940; a documented pre-war Soviet espionage role; and a Soviet Embassy contact in January 1940.

This is still a hypothesis. The decisive source is the declassified MI5 file set **KV 2/992–996**, especially 1940 surveillance/contact entries.
