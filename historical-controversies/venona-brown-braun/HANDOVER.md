## Latest Session – 2026-09-05

### Summary of work done

Opened the VENONA BROWN/BRAUN identity problem as a cracker target and anchored it in the three primary 1940 London GRU cables. The first pass deliberately separated **BROWN's identity** from the **Henry Hughes technical source**, then used patent/technical evidence to narrow the latter.

The best new lead is Donald Orr Sproule: a Canadian Henry Hughes engineer whose pre-war patents sit directly in the echo-sounding/directional-sound technology family and who is later described in a scholarly technical history as having been forced out of Kelvin Hughes over alleged Russian-spy associations. That allegation is presently unverified at primary-source level.

### What worked / partial results worth keeping

1. **Role separation.** The Sep 1940 cable does not say BROWN worked at Henry Hughes. Maintain insider vs handler vs CP-network-collector models.
2. **Technical narrowing.** The “horizontal echo-sounder” clue maps to a small group of Henry Hughes echo/acoustics personnel rather than the whole factory.
3. **Sproule lead.** Strong technical/source fit plus a later espionage allegation makes his file the highest-information archival target.
4. **Message-number cleanup.** “No. 256” is almost certainly a sequential local dispatch number, because the prior message is “No. 255 BARCh.” Do not treat it as an agent ID.
5. **Source trap removed.** Vassiliev's “Braun” search hit belongs to a US/NKGB network, not London GRU.

### What failed and why

- Public web searching did not reveal a direct current identification of BROWN.
- Generic searches for Communist links to the known Henry Hughes patent inventors produced no reliable evidence.
- The UK National Archives web index did not return a clean standalone Donald Orr Sproule result. A secondary SOE index points to HS 9/1401, but the exact sub-file and contents are not yet verified.
- The 1998 “Russian spies” statement about Sproule is not enough on its own: its visible text does not show a source citation for that sentence.

### Recommended next experiments

1. **Highest priority — verify Sproule's security-file trail.** Obtain/catalogue-check Donald Orr Sproule in TNA HS 9/1401 and search KV files for SPROULE, HENRY HUGHES and KELVIN HUGHES. Determine exactly why/when the Russian-spy allegation arose and whether it names contacts.
2. **Build the 1939–40 echo-sounder team roster.** Enumerate Henry Hughes people tied to GB591533A, US2350080A, US2076330A, Type 758N / horizontal ASDIC work, production testing and Admiralty contracts. Separate inventors from actual production-line/test staff.
3. **Identify the Communist Party Area Secretary in the relevant locality in Aug 1940.** Focus Hainault/Ilford/Forest Gate/East London party structure. Then inspect their known contacts for Henry Hughes workers and radio people.
4. **Residence attack.** For each serious candidate, reconstruct 1939–40 addresses and ask whether a move back to an “old flat” in Aug 1940 is visible in electoral/MI5/1939 Register evidence.
5. **Technical fingerprinting.** Determine whether the cable description matches one specific Hughes device/project more closely than the general public echo-sounder patents. If yes, use that project's staff list as the source pool.
6. **Network expansion only after identities are grounded.** Try to identify POULTRY-DEALER and MINISTR from independent evidence; either may expose BROWN through contact chains.

### New leads or related problems discovered

- The 1954/55 patent litigation records a Henry Hughes licensing agreement dated **17 April 1940** covering the distance-by-echo patent but excluding the receiving/transmitting oscillator. This may help identify what technology was commercially or militarily sensitive around the exact period of the BROWN report.
- Sproule appears in a secondary index of SOE personnel files with birth date **26 May 1903** and HS 9/1401. If genuine, the reason he has an SOE personnel file may itself be highly informative.
- The later engineer **Tom Brown** replacing Sproule is almost certainly a coincidence unless independent evidence says otherwise; keep it quarantined as a potential source-confusion mechanism in later histories.

- Was BROWN technically trained, or purely a trusted intermediary?
- Which CPGB “Area” is meant in No. 876?
