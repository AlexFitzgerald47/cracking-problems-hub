### Current state

The problem has advanced beyond the earlier Fraser-only model. The strongest current reconstruction is:

### Why Meredith moved to #1

1. Meredith was a **proven pre-war GRU technical source**, not merely a Communist sympathizer.
2. Vernon — now the strongest POULTRY-DEALER candidate because of his exact Wintringham/Osterley, Hounslow, CPGB and Soviet-intelligence fit — had a direct operational relationship with Meredith. Released-file reconstructions report that Vernon said **Meredith recruited him** into the Soviet espionage activity; Vernon later consulted Meredith before finalising his MI5 account.
3. Meredith left the RAE in 1938 to become **Chief Designer / head of Physics and Instruments at Smiths Aircraft Instruments, Cricklewood**.
4. Smiths had acquired a **controlling interest in Henry Hughes & Son in 1935** and its aircraft/marine organization operated with the Henry Hughes subsidiary. This is the first hard institutional bridge between a known GRU source and the exact company named in BROWN’s 4 Sep 1940 report.
5. Meredith’s known intelligence activity was technical/military collection, matching the category of No. 976.
6. The July/August “new flat” → “old flat” sequence is structurally compatible with an existing source given temporary operational accommodation. Meredith had a normal pre-war residence at **Old Brew House, St Albans** while working in Cricklewood; by May 1941 he was at Bishop’s Cleeve with Smiths’ wartime relocation. Exact Aug 1940 residence remains unresolved.
7. A final direct search for published BROWN/BRAUN mappings produced **no competing identification and no contradiction**. Published material continues to place Meredith and Vernon together in the relevant pre-war GRU network but does not explicitly map Meredith to BROWN.

### Why Vernon remains the best POULTRY-DEALER candidate

- Proven pre-war Soviet/GRU source.
- MI5 observed him lunching with Soviet Embassy second secretary Ivan Popov in Jan 1940.
- Directly involved with **Tom Wintringham at Osterley Park in July 1940**; POULTRY-DEALER reports on Wintringham’s special Home Guard school on 14 Aug.
- Osterley/Hounslow geography matches POULTRY-DEALER’s later reporting on Eastern Command’s Hounslow move.
- Had real CPGB organizational connections, including a described role as a cipher/contact link between the RAE area and Party HQ.
- Had direct operational history with Meredith.

## Latest Session – 2026-09-05 continuation

### Summary of work done

The problem moved from a one-name technical lead to a **two-role identity hypothesis**.

Primary VENONA re-reading shows that the same POULTRY-DEALER who reports the BROWN/W/T/Communist Party security problem on **13 Aug 1940** reports on **Tom Wintringham and the International Brigade political milieu on 14 Aug**. This independently points BROWN's network toward CPGB/International Brigade circles.

A first serious BROWN candidate emerged: **Harry (Henry Balfour) Fraser**, a lifelong CPGB member and International Brigader who deliberately trained in electronics, worked in the Ilford electronics industry after Spain, had a documented personal route through Dave Springhall, and personally knew Len Beurton — later a Soviet wireless operator. Fraser also has a known old Hackney address before later staying in Ilford, making No. 876's “move to his old flat” a concrete falsifiable residence test.

Separately, technical fingerprinting strengthened the Sproule/Hughes source lead: the **1939 GB908039A / later GB591533A directional-sound apparatus** used a motor-driven rotatable acoustic reflector, including a two-axis version. It predates the Sep 1940 leak but was not published until 1947 and is a much tighter engineering match to a “horizontal echo-sounder” than ordinary downward depth sounding.

The leading model is therefore now:

> **H2/H3:** Fraser is a plausible candidate for **BROWN the CPGB intermediary/collector**, while Sproule or another worker on the Sproule–Hughes directional-acoustics project is the **Henry Hughes source**.

This is **not a solve claim**. The load-bearing Fraser ↔ Henry Hughes/project bridge has not been found.

### What worked / partial results worth keeping

1. **POULTRY-DEALER network fingerprint:** consecutive 13–14 Aug cables put BROWN and an International Brigade/Home Guard story in the same reporter's information sphere.
2. **Harry Fraser candidate:** first person found who independently fits several BROWN-side constraints rather than only the Henry Hughes clue.
3. **Springhall edge:** Fraser personally used Dave Springhall to get into Spain; Springhall later became National Organiser and a proven collector of classified material with Soviet-intelligence implications.
4. **Beurton edge:** Fraser's daughter states Harry knew Len Beurton from the British Battalion; TNA independently identifies Beurton as a Soviet-network wireless operator.
5. **Technical project fingerprint:** GB908039A's steerable reflector is an unusually close engineering fit to “horizontal echo-sounder.”
6. **Employer correction:** accessible biography does not establish Plessey 1939–45; it says Plessey after Spain and later wartime radar work at Cossor. Exact 1940 chronology must be settled.
7. **Interview pinned:** Tameside Manchester Studies Collection tree lists **GB131.1103 item 207, International Brigade interview with Mr Harry Fraser**. Item-level web route currently fails.

### What failed and why

- No direct Fraser ↔ Henry Hughes, Fraser ↔ Sproule, or Fraser ↔ GB908039A-project contact found.
- No direct evidence yet that Fraser personally handled clandestine W/T, despite strong electronics and wireless-network adjacency.
- No exact Aug 1940 Fraser residence found; the old-flat match remains a prediction.
- No exact 1940 Ilford Communist Party “Area Secretary” identified. Ted Bramley is documented as an Ilford CP organiser before Spain, but that is too early to equate him with the 1940 Area Secretary.
- Sproule's later “Russian spies” allegation still lacks an independent documentary source and should be **downgraded to archival pointer only**.
- Tameside's collection-level catalogue exposes item 207, but the direct item page errors and no audio/transcript was retrievable remotely this pass.

### Recommended next experiments

1. **Residence kill test on Fraser.** Resolve 1939–41 electoral-register/address evidence for 30 Millfields Road, Hackney and his Ilford residence. The Aug 1940 “old flat” move is the cleanest candidate-specific falsifier currently available.
2. **Get the Fraser oral history.** Inspect Tameside GB131.1103/207 and/or the 1976 IWM Margaret A. Brooks interview for exact Plessey→Cossor dates, residences, wireless/radio activity and named party contacts.
3. **Springhall cross-check.** Search TNA KV 2/1594–1598 and KV 2/2063–2065 for Fraser, Plessey, Ilford, Henry Hughes, Cossor, Spanish comrades and industrial information sources.
4. **Build the GB908039A project roster.** Identify draughtsmen, test staff, production workers and Admiralty contacts around Sproule/A. J. Hughes, not only patent inventors.
5. **Find the bridge.** Intersect that roster with Ilford/Hainault CPGB, International Brigade, electrical-union and Fraser/Con Harvey social records.
6. **Identify POULTRY-DEALER / 1940 Area Secretary if possible.** Either identity could collapse the BROWN candidate set rapidly.
7. **Locate Admiralty/project files.** Test whether a Sproule/Hughes steerable-reflector prototype was actually in production/testing by summer 1940; patent filing alone is not proof of production.
