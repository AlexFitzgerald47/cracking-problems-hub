## State in one paragraph

German Enigma traffic, known from at least two released documents: London No. 649 of
3 April 1941 and a two-page "Report from BARON" of 29 July 1941. Nigel West identified him
as the Czechoslovak intelligence officer Karel Sedláček; Haynes's concordance carries that
identification, attributed to West. The identification is untested and collides with
Sedláček's residence in Switzerland. This session could not read any of the primary
documents and therefore scored no candidates; it produced a solution-status audit, a
labelled constraint ledger, a preregistered hypothesis about the 3 April 1941 date
