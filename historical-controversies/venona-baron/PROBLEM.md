# VENONA BARON — identify the London GRU source who reported on British ENIGMA decryption

## Statement

Identify the real person behind the Soviet military-intelligence covername **BARON** in the
1941 London GRU VENONA traffic — the source whose reporting is connected in the public
record with **British decryption of German Enigma traffic** — or reduce the identity to a
small, defensible candidate set and adjudicate the one identification already in print.

At least two public BARON documents exist in the NSA VENONA releases:

- **London No. 649, 3 April 1941** — the message in which BARON's Enigma-related reporting
  appears. Secondary descriptions state the cable says the information came *solely* from
  BARON, references ENIGMA, and notes the source's possible connection with Military
  Intelligence.
- **"Report from BARON", 29 July 1941** — a two-page NSA release (Release 5).

Both are catalogued below. **Neither was readable during the session that opened this
problem** — see `analysis/access-ledger.md`. Everything in this folder derived from those
cables is therefore second-hand and marked as such.

## Why it belongs on the board

This is the highest-consequence unresolved identity in the public London GRU corpus. If
BARON reported to Moscow that British warnings about German intentions were derived from
**cryptanalysis of Enigma**, then the Soviet Union knew in 1941 the single fact British
intelligence was most determined to conceal — the fact Churchill personally disguised when
he warned Stalin on 3 April 1941 by attributing the intelligence to "a trusted agent".

That makes BARON a different kind of target from an ordinary covername. The historical
payload is not "one more Soviet agent" but **whether the security architecture around Ultra
was penetrated at the provenance level in the first half of 1941, and by whom** — with
direct consequences for the long argument about what Stalin knew before Barbarossa and why
he discounted it.

It also passes the Hub's crack-fit test. The named unknown is finite. Candidates can lose:
a candidate must have had access to the *provenance* of Enigma-derived intelligence in
London in early 1941, a channel to the GRU rather than the NKVD, and a chronology
consistent with both dated documents. And there is a standing published identification to
beat or break.

## Solution status — read this before working the problem

**BARON is not cleanly unidentified, and it is not cleanly identified either.** The Hub's
2026-09-05 frontier-model pass ranked BARON the number-one Dominic target on the premise
that "the NSA's VENONA history still describes BARON as unidentified". That premise is
incomplete. A published identification exists:

- **Nigel West**, *VENONA: The Greatest Secret of the Cold War* (1999), pp. 67–69, identifies
  BARON as **Karel Sedláček**, the Czechoslovak military-intelligence officer stationed in
  Switzerland who was the London Czechs' link to Rudolf Roessler ("Lucy").
- That identification is carried in **John Earl Haynes's standard cover-name concordance** as
  `"Baron": Sedlacek, Karel (U.K. line, [West Venona])` — where the bracket attributes the
  identification to West rather than to an archival source.

Against it: at least one secondary account renders the identification as *probable* rather
than settled, and an unnamed MI5 officer is quoted in 1997 saying no serious attempt had
ever been made to identify BARON. The identification also collides with a hard geographical
constraint — Sedláček was resident in **Switzerland**, not Britain, from 1939 onward.

So the problem stands, but **restated**: it is now an adjudication of a named hypothesis,
not an open-field search. See `analysis/solution-status-ledger.md`. Anyone who works this
without engaging West's identification is re-running the DAN mistake this board has already
recorded once.

## The central ambiguity

Three readings of BARON's Enigma reporting compete, and they demand different people:

- **H1 — Provenance leak from inside the British Ultra circle.** BARON knew that the
  intelligence Britain was passing to Moscow came from breaking Enigma, and told Moscow so.
  This requires indoctrinated or near-indoctrinated access in London in early 1941, when
  the circle was very small.
- **H2 — Czech channel.** BARON was an officer of Czechoslovak military intelligence — which
  is *literally* "Military Intelligence", matching the annotator's note — reporting material
  that reached the Czechs from British sources. West's Sedláček identification is a special
  case of this.
- **H3 — Product without provenance.** BARON supplied German operational intelligence that
  happened to be Enigma-derived, and the ENIGMA reference is the VENONA annotator's or
  Moscow's inference rather than BARON's own claim.

H1 and H3 are separated by exactly one question about the cable: **does BARON say where the
material came from, or does someone else say it?** That question is answerable from two
pages of released document, and it is the highest-information cheap test on the board for
this problem.

## Success criteria

### Full crack
A named individual who independently satisfies the access, channel, chronology and
service-affiliation constraints from both dated documents, with documentary evidence tying
that person to the London GRU residency and explaining how they came to know the provenance
of British Enigma-derived intelligence.

### Strong partial crack
Either (a) demonstrate that West's Sedláček identification is or is not supported by the
traffic, with the reasoning shown; or (b) establish from the primary documents which of
H1/H2/H3 the cables actually support, which collapses most of the candidate universe.

### Falsifiable candidate standard
A candidate must satisfy:
- present and active in the relevant theatre in April **and** July 1941 (both documents);
- a documented channel to Soviet **military** intelligence (GRU), not the NKVD residency —
  Blunt/Cairncross-type NKVD routes do not satisfy this without evidence of cross-service
  passage;
- access consistent with whichever of H1/H2/H3 the cables support;
- no contradiction from residence, posting, service record or security file;
- an independent corroborating record outside VENONA.
