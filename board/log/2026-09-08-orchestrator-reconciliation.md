## Practices distilled

- Freeze inscription topology as a graph before serialising a forked/detached object.
- Audit metadata semantics and true observational units before calling a cell controlled.
- Compare covariate holdouts with size-matched per-class ablation.
- Determine scope direction and separators before semantic or arithmetic interpretation.
- Normalise historical spelling before historical authorship attribution, with frozen
  rules and an unnormalised sensitivity result.
- Never promote informal `SOLVED` language past the solve-claim/validator/human gates.
