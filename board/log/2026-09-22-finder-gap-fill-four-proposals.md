## Verification note

Every load-bearing source for all four proposals was independently fetched or searched by the
coordinating session itself, not taken on a research subagent's word — this caught two errors
before they landed (a wrong publisher guess for a 1984 book, and an unverifiable "flat
frequency" claim for the cipher corpus that isn't actually on the primary transcription page).
Anything that couldn't be confirmed this way is marked unverified in place in the relevant
`PROBLEM.md`, per the brief's rule that marking something unverified is always acceptable and
asserting it is not.

Two strong candidates were investigated, confirmed genuinely open, and deliberately held over
rather than written up this run (one Ireland authorship case, a Genghis Khan misquote citation
chain, and an Old Turkic runiform sign-value cluster) — see the manifest for why, so a future
run does not have to re-discover them from scratch.
