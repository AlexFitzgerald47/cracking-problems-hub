## Findings

**The decisive source has been strengthened in retelling.** Langlois p.43 describes the Academy-squeeze recesses as possibly engraved and expressly retains uncertainty about taw. His p.45 discussion still entertains `BNDWD`; it does not report eliminating nun. The images make the taw proposal intelligible, but I cannot classify all disputed traces as original incision from these selected renderings. His description establishes another squeeze at the Academy, but this passage alone does not establish the claimant's stronger provenance claim that it is an independently made pre-break impression. That provenance needs documenting, not assuming. [1]

**The opposing case is evidentiary, not merely a demand for pristine letters.** Richelle and Burlingame dispute whether the central traces represent letters rather than deformation, discuss examination of both squeeze surfaces and RTI, and dispute the terminal divider. Consequently, requiring critics to supply a better complete word misstates their alternative: the surviving evidence may underdetermine the missing letters. Their argument remains compatible with `BTDWD` being plausible. [2]

**The repository does not document execution of its own outward experiment.** The first analysis already discusses crossing traces on the Academy squeeze. Its preregistered follow-up instead asks for persistence under independent illumination, comparison with secure taws, separation of the first dalet from folds, and a topographic divider control. The solve push cites the same published study; it supplies no new blind tracing, illumination series, comparanda measurements, or controlled boundary analysis. Revisiting an existing figure can improve an interpretation, but it is not the newly completed raw-evidence test the handover describes.

**The claimed holdout is not demonstrated.** Tel Dan is a separate artefact, but the claimant and its source knew the proposed David reading before the claimed freeze. Putting lexical discussion later in a document does not establish blinded adjudication or held-out prediction. The parallel can support interpretation conditional on the letters without independently confirming which marks are letters.

**A weak rival does not prove the winner.** Unobserved `LQ` prevents treating Balak as an observed reading. It does not physically contradict every restoration containing those consonants. Likewise, failure to demonstrate a pre-W separator is not proof of its absence. I therefore do not independently certify even a complete Balak-branch elimination under the strong-partial criterion. PARTIAL here records useful evidence assessment; it is not certification of that narrower success criterion.

## Decision and next gate

Retain `BTDWD` as a serious proposed reconstruction; do not promote it to a solved reading. The missing criterion is independently established stroke evidence and a demonstrated comparative advantage, not unanimity or absolute certainty. Failure to reproduce that evidence is sufficient to withhold PASS; a validator need not first invent a superior complete word.

A useful next session should obtain squeeze provenance and raw or sufficiently varied imaging, freeze ambiguous marks without lexical labels, compare the second-position traces with secure taws/nuns and damage controls, and independently test the first dalet and terminal divider. Record ambiguous outcomes as ambiguous. Until then, the first pass's qualified `B??WD` frame is safer, with its boundary assumptions explicitly retained.

Limitations: no raw RTI, no object inspection, no independent full reading of Finkelstein/Na'aman/Römer or the syntax corpus, and no calibrated confidence score. I have not established that `BTDWD` is false.
