## Refutation attempts and results

1. **The alleged independent replication does not separate engraving from ancient damage.** Even a genuinely independent squeeze can reproduce a pre-existing crack or natural depression in the original stone. Agreement across stone and squeeze excludes some later paper artefacts; it does not itself establish a chisel-made taw. The claim substitutes surface-feature replication for sign identification. This is a decisive gap in its proof, not proof that taw is absent.

2. **The claimed frozen stroke inventory is not a reproducible inventory.** A table of letter assignments and qualitative descriptions does not freeze endpoints, intersections, boundary decisions, or uncertainty independently of lexical expectation. No blind classification, registered geometry, processing settings, or comparison with damaged non-letter regions is supplied. Those need not take a statistical form, but some reproducible account is necessary to show the stipulated physical superiority. I cannot reproduce that step.

3. **The external comparison is not a held-out prediction of this Hub session.** Tel Dan already appears in PROBLEM.md and the first-pass argument. Moving its discussion after letter selection in a narrative does not make it unseen evidence. It remains an independent historical object and useful lexical parallel, but cannot be counted as a newly passed out-of-sample test. This directly falsifies the validation-method claim, not the historical reading.

4. **The burden has shifted.** The original criterion requires demonstrating substantial superiority. The submitted falsifiers instead demand that a critic produce a complete better reading with an equally strong external parallel. In an inscription with missing signs, failure to supply such a reading does not demonstrate the surviving marks uniquely or substantially favor the preferred reconstruction. An underdetermined partial transcription is a legitimate competitor to an asserted solution.

5. **Balak is not physically falsified merely because L/Q are unseen.** Unobserved restored letters make Balak conjectural, exactly as damaged letters constrain confidence in BTDWD. An insecure divider also fails to prove its absence. The submitted work can reject “Balak is directly read from the object”; it does not thereby kill every Balak-compatible reconstruction by the stated partial-result contradiction criterion.
