## Why the Meredith case is materially stronger than prior candidates

- Meredith was a **proven pre-war Soviet military-intelligence/GRU technical source**.
- Vernon and Meredith were members of the same Soviet espionage network; released-file reconstructions report Vernon saying **Meredith recruited him**.
- Vernon independently fits POULTRY-DEALER’s 1940 reporting fingerprint unusually well: direct Tom Wintringham/Osterley involvement in July 1940, Hounslow geography, CPGB organization, Soviet Embassy contact in Jan 1940, and prior Soviet-intelligence work.
- Meredith became **Chief Designer / head of Physics and Instruments at Smiths Aircraft Instruments in 1938**.
- Smiths had acquired a **controlling interest in Henry Hughes & Son in 1935** and its aviation/marine structure operated with the Hughes subsidiary. This provides the first hard institutional pathway from a known GRU technical source to the exact company named in BROWN’s 4 Sep 1940 report.
- The July/August cable sequence itself predicts temporary operational accommodation followed by return to an older residence; Meredith had a known 1939 St Albans home while working in Cricklewood and later moved with Smiths’ wartime organization toward Bishop’s Cleeve.
- Direct searches for published BROWN/BRAUN mappings or contradictory Meredith chronology produced no competing identification or hard contradiction.
