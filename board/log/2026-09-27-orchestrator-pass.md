## Has anything been solved?

**No internal claim has a PASS verdict.** Every completed validator verdict is PARTIAL. Four
claims are HELD after complete three-validator panels: Ennis STINGING, Mesha BTDWD, VENONA
Meredith/Vernon and the Chinese gold bars. Linear A and Byblos have bounded claims but no panel.

The only clean “solved” event is defensive: the proposed Ormonde–Maltravers cipher target was
withdrawn when the finder discovered Daniel Bourdeau's external solution. That is good discovery
hygiene, not a Hub solve.

Several projects have answered a narrower question strongly enough to stop repeating it:

- **Templo Mayor:** 80,400 is best explained as a transmitted vigesimal unit-expression, not a
  literal-looking tally residue. The transmission map still depends on Motolinía's 1555 letter and
  direct inspection of the Telleriano-Remensis folio.
- **Thera:** the radiocarbon success criterion is answered by an information bound; IntCal20
  cannot resolve the plateau interior at any sample size. A better annual curve could reopen it.
- **Blood eagle:** the evidence is undecidable for a measured reason—the key stanza is a corpus
  hapax. The load-bearing adjudication and dating assumptions still deserve one independent audit.
- **Caligula:** the corpus strongly favours literal shell-gathering over the proposed lexical
  emendation, but Woods 2000 and Malloch 2001 remain unread, so the reading side is not closed.
- **Junius:** current cross-register stylometry cannot rank Francis; the gap exceeds the author
  signal and is mostly author-specific. This is a useful negative, not an attribution.
- **Shakespeare:** the cross-register correction replicated out of sample. It validates an
  instrument, not an authorship claimant; Oxford/Bacon/Derby remain out of scope.
