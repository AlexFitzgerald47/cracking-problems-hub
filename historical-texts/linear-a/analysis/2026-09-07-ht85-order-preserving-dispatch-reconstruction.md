## Why HT85 can be pushed further

Existing scholarship already gets unusually close to a functional reading:

- side a contributes 66 people from the first sector of the 19-node Haghia Triada circuit;
- 66 resolves into eleven groups of six;
- side b has eleven unit entries;
- Younger tentatively assigns two groups to QE-KA, three to TE-TU, and one group to each of the other six entries;
- Davis & Valério suggest the tablet records movement of 66 people from circuit entities 1-7 to entities 8-13 plus KI-RE-TA2 and QE-KA.

The missing step is an actual source -> receiving-party dispatch schedule.

## The leap

Assume the tablet preserves **clerical order through the transfer**: expand side-a contributions into consecutive six-person gang slots in written order, expand side-b responsibility entries into consecutive gang slots in written order, then pair slot 1 with slot 1, slot 2 with slot 2, etc.

This is the minimum-crossing / order-preserving assignment. It is not proven, but it is the least arbitrary way to turn both ordered ledgers into a transport map.

## Historical interpretation

If this ordering assumption is correct, HT85 is not merely a movement record. It is a **brigade-formation and deployment sheet**:

1. local accountable nodes owe different manpower quotas;
2. quotas are aggregated into standardized six-person gangs;
3. small source quotas are pooled to make whole gangs;
4. gangs are allocated in ordered succession to receiving/supervising entries;
5. larger receiving entities (QE-KA, TE-TU) control multiple gangs and possess subordinate functionaries.

That implies a two-stage labor bureaucracy:

`LOCAL LEVY -> CENTRAL GANG FORMATION -> ASSIGNMENT / SUPERVISION`.

The distinction matters. A simple conscription list says who owes people. HT85 appears capable of showing **how the administration transformed local obligations into deployable work units**.

## Strongest social-organizational inference

The pooled tail `5 + 3 + 4 = 12` is the most informative detail. It means the source quota was not necessarily itself defined in six-person units. The six-person unit appears at the **mobilization / deployment layer**, because three small contributors can jointly form two standard crews.

That suggests:

- source communities/institutions were assessed in individual persons;
- the central administration created standardized work gangs after collection;
- recipients/supervisors were assigned whole gangs.

This is administratively more sophisticated than a flat corvée roster.
