ADJUDICATION OF THE ONE-SIGN TOKENS -- read this before believing any P1 rate.
A one-sign 'word' in an administrative syllabary is usually an ABBREVIATION, not
a word, and counting them manufactures a confirmation of P1. Inspected by hand:

  LA  1,101 one-sign tokens, dominated by KU (170), KA (169), SI (118), RO (95),
      NI (76), TE (58), ZE (47).  These are the standard Linear A single-syllabogram
      transaction terms and commodity designators -- NI is the conventional sign for
      figs.  They are not words.  Because Linear A is undeciphered I cannot separate
      abbreviation from word on any principled basis, so LA IS DISQUALIFIED as a
      comparison for P1/P3, and its apparent PASS must NOT be counted.

  CY  Latin editorial words (linea, vacat, evanidus, illegibilis) leaked into the
      first run and are now excluded.  The remainder -- to (32), a, o, ti, u -- are
      genuine Greek function words spelled syllabically, and DO count.

  LB  24 one-sign tokens out of 7,701.  Some of these are probably abbreviations
      too, which would push the rate even lower.  The direction is against P1.
