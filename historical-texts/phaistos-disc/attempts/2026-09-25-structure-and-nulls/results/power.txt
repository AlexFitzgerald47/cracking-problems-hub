Phaistos Disc: power analysis

Q1  H1 power
    null distribution of S (stratified): mean 0.653, P(S>=3)=0.01930, P(S>=4)=0.00156, P(S>=5)=0.00005
    critical S at the budget-charged 4e-4 threshold: S >= 5
    critical S at a nominal 0.01:                    S >= 4
    observed S = 5, so the test had margin: it would still have fired at S = 5.

    power to detect a closed section-final repertoire of R types,
    where a fraction r of the 18 marked slots is drawn from it (10000 sims):
      r      R=2     R=3     R=4     R=6
      0.3   0.289   0.165   0.099   0.037 
      0.5   0.881   0.778   0.670   0.434 
      0.7   0.999   0.995   0.981   0.933 
      1.0   1.000   1.000   1.000   1.000 
    Read: the test is well powered for a small closed repertoire and loses
    power fast as R grows. At R = 6 with only 30%% of slots formulaic it is blind.

Q2  positional restriction on signs
    a sign occurring f times has f chances to be group-initial; with 61 groups
    and mean group length 3.95 the marginal P(initial) = 0.253
    signs and their initial/total counts (top by frequency):
       sign 02  total 19  initial 19  final  0
       sign 07  total 18  initial  4  final  8
       sign 12  total 17  initial  0  final  4
       sign 27  total 15  initial  5  final  2
       sign 18  total 12  initial  0  final  5
       sign 01  total 11  initial  1  final  7
       sign 29  total 11  initial  8  final  0
       sign 35  total 11  initial  0  final  7
       sign 23  total 11  initial  1  final  1
       sign 25  total  7  initial  0  final  3

    a STRICTLY group-initial sign is detectable at p<0.01 once it occurs f times,
    where P(all f initial by chance) = 0.253^f < 0.01  ->  f >= 4
    signs with at least that many occurrences: 25 of 45
    So a positional grammar can only ever be demonstrated for the handful of
    high-frequency signs. For the 17 signs occurring once or twice it is
    UNTESTABLE on this corpus, not absent.

Q3  the ceiling on decipherment from this corpus alone
    45 sign types over 241 tokens; 9 hapaxes and 17 signs occurring <= 2 times.
    Any phonetic value assigned to a hapax rests on ONE attestation and cannot be
    cross-checked internally at any significance level. That is 17 of 45 signs
    (38% of the signary) permanently unconstrained by internal evidence.
    Consequence, as a bound rather than a test: a self-consistent reading of the
    Disc is not evidence of a correct reading, because 38% of the signary can be
    set freely without creating a single internal contradiction.
    Free parameters in a syllabic reading: 45 sign values.
    Internal consistency constraints available: 7 repeated group types + 188
    That is fewer constraints than parameters, which is why readability cannot
    discriminate. Matches the board's short-cipher validation bound.
