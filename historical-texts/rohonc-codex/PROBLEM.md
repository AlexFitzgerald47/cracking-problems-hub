# Rohonc Codex

## Statement
Decipher the Rohonc Codex: identify the script, the underlying language (or encoding), and produce a coherent reading of the text. Explain the relationship between the text and the accompanying illustrations.

## Why it belongs on the board
A genuine historical codex written in an unknown script. Contains religious and military illustrations. Has resisted decipherment despite multiple serious attempts. Provenance is Hungarian / Central European.

## Known constraints / previous major attempts
- Various claims of solution (Hungarian, Romanian, artificial language, cipher, etc.) none of which has gained broad acceptance.
- The script has a large character set.
- Codicological and art-historical studies provide chronological and cultural constraints.

## Success criteria
A reproducible decipherment that yields meaningful, consistent text across multiple pages and aligns with the illustrations and historical context.

## Key sources & starting points
- High-quality images of the codex
- Existing character inventories and statistical studies
- Scholarly literature on its provenance and previous decipherment attempts
