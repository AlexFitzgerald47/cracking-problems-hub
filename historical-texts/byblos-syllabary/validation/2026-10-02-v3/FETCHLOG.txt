schmutz_maeder_2024.pdf / .txt
  https://center-for-decipherment.ch/journal/2024_01__Schmutz-%26-Maeder__Die-Byblos-Schrift_Beurteilung-Woudhuizen-Best.pdf
  Confirms the kernel's section 5 reporting.  Two further things the kernel does
  not report: (a) Mendenhall is handled in footnote 33 only, by asserting that
  Colless explains the mismatch away with "diversen schlecht verstaendlichen
  Hilfshypothesen" -- the application of Mendenhall's values to the three names

  deferred to a later paper.  Garbini 2009 is therefore a live rival system and
  is absent from the Hub's "what is already dead" section.
