N1.  VARIANT DEPENDENCE OF THE SPLIT
==============================================================================
  The merged group that the kernel wants split.  In the default/search
  inventory and in syl1-syl5, the group containing E4AF is:
    search  group = E416 E495 E4AE E4AF
    syl1    group = E416 E495 E4AE E4AF E4FD
    syl2    group = E416 E495 E4AE E4AF E4FD
    syl3    group = E416 E495 E4AE E4AF E4FD
    syl4    group = E416 E495 E4AE E4AF E4FD
    syl5    group = E416 E495 E4AE E4AF E4FD
    syl6    group = E450 E455 E47B E4C9 E4AE E4AF E4FD
    syl7    group = E450 E455 E47B E4C9 E4AE E4AF E4FD
    syl8    group = E450 E455 E47B E4C9 E4AE E4AF E4FD

  Now apply the kernel's own inference -- 'two members of one merged
  group inside one four-sign name is an unnecessary polyfunctionality,
  therefore split them' -- to each OCBI reading of rb and rc:
    rb (Var. 1)    class 25 holds E4AE+E4AF
    rb (Var. 2)    class 25 holds E4AE+E4AF
    rb (Var. 3)    class 25 holds E416+E4AF
    rc (Var. 1)    no two distinct forms of one class
    rc (Var. 2)    class 28 holds E4B1+E4B2
    rc (Var. 3)    no two distinct forms of one class

  -> rb Var. 1 and Var. 2 (E4FF E49A E4AE E4AF) put E4AE and E4AF -- two
     forms OCBI names with the SAME motif ('Sonnenaufgang' / 'Sonnenaufgang / Rechts-Schlaufe')
     inside the same four-sign name.  The kernel's inference, applied to
     those readings, would demand E4AE != E4AF, a DIFFERENT split, and
     one that cuts across OCBI's own motif naming.
  -> rc Var. 2 (E4B0 E4B1 E4B2 E4FD) does not end in E4AF at all; its
     terminal E4FD is another member of the same merged group, so under
     that reading the two sisters do NOT share 'the exact same terminal
     sign' and constraint (3) of kernel section 2 is false.
  The conclusion is therefore not identified by the evidence: which
  grapheme pair gets split is decided by which of the nine OCBI readings
  is adopted, and the kernel adopts Var.3/Var.3 without argument.

N3.  THE KERNEL'S OWN ANCHORS BREAK THE SAME RULE
==============================================================================
  OCBI syllableMap (the only machine-readable sound map in the source):
     me     E49A   das Z gerundet
     pa     E44D   d XXVIII 2  Andreaskreuz
     ATON   E416    offener Ring
     AMUN   E42A E483   d III 11  Joch / Kamm mit 3 Zähnen
     i      E42A   d III 11  Joch
     o      E45A   d XXXIX 8  Torbogen mit Querstrich
     ḥ      E419   Ochs
     m      E41F   hängender Links-Haken mit Decke
     b      E444   d XV 10 Kranich ohne Flügel
     ś      E411   Torbogen
     h      E410   eckige Spirale
     aleph  E447   d XVI 10 Schilf

  E42A is mapped to the single value 'i' AND is the first half of the
  two-sign logogram 'AMUN'.  Off-cylinder it is the #14 most frequent
  form in the corpus (23 tokens of 1302).
  So the kernel accepts, inside the six-sign name ra, exactly the
  polyfunctionality it refuses to accept inside the four-sign name rb.
  E483 off-cylinder tokens: 2

  The kernel further declares the source's 'ATON E416' mapping 'stale
  residue'.  Two things to note:
    * E416 is named ' offener Ring' -- an open ring, i.e. a plausible
      sun-disc; E4AF is named 'Sonnenaufgang / Rechts-Schlaufe'.
      Colless (2019) independently identifies the sun sign on this seal
      as 'a circle with a dot in it' in the SECOND position of the long
      column, i.e. E4AD (Auge klein), not as either.
    * if ATON = E416, then rb Var.3 reads me-ATON-?-? and the kernel's
      alignment fails.  The kernel resolves a conflict between its
      alignment and the only machine-readable sound map in its primary
      source by declaring the source stale.  That is an assertion, not
      an external adjudication.  (The GEAS 2021 news page does give
      'E4AF ATON', so GEAS is internally inconsistent with its own tool;
      the kernel's report of the GEAS page is accurate.)

  -> E4AF itself is cylinder-only (as the kernel says), and so are E4AE
     and E4FD: all three members of the 'Sonnenaufgang/Boot' end of the
     merged group occur ONLY on the seal.  The 20 off-cylinder tokens the
     merged class had were almost all E416 (17) plus E495 (3).
  -> So the split's whole effect is to strip the one well-attested form
     (E416) out of the ATON class, leaving an ATON class with 3
     off-cylinder tokens under syl6-syl8.  That is a real change to the
     inventory, but it makes the ATON anchor LESS testable off the
     cylinder, not more: it generates no cross-text prediction at all,
     and it is unfalsifiable from the Dunand core by construction.
