K0.  WHAT THE SEAL ACTUALLY OFFERS  (primary source, not the write-up)
==============================================================================
  OCBI records 8 entries for the seal, i.e. 4 physical columns with
  alternative readings:
    ra               n=6  E4AC E4AD E41F E44D E42A E483
    rb (Var. 1)      n=4  E4FF E49A E4AE E4AF
    rb (Var. 2)      n=4  E4FF E49A E4AE E4AF
    rb (Var. 3)      n=4  E49A E416 E491 E4AF
    rc (Var. 1)      n=5  E4B0 E42A E4AE E417 E4AE
    rc (Var. 2)      n=4  E4B0 E4B1 E4B2 E4FD
    rc (Var. 3)      n=4  E4B0 E443 E429 E4AF
    rd               n=3  E45C E4B3 E429

  -> the composition is said to carry THREE daughter names, but OCBI
     transcribes FOUR columns (ra, rb, rc, rd).  Column rd is left
     unaccounted for by the alignment; choosing which 3 of 4 columns to
     use is a free parameter the kernel never charges.
  -> rb has 3 alternative readings, rc has 3.  9 combinations.

  off-cylinder canonical corpus: 1302 sign tokens, 211 distinct forms
  P(two independently drawn signs are the SAME form)  = sum p_i^2 = 0.01306

  licence (b) same grapheme class in the DEFAULT OCBI inventory:

K3.  MONTE CARLO OVER THE SEAL, CHARGING THE VARIANT BUDGET
==============================================================================
  Re-draw the seal as 4 columns of the observed lengths (6,4,4,3) with
  signs i.i.d. from off-cylinder frequencies.  For rb and rc draw 3
  alternative readings each, exactly as OCBI offers.  Then ask whether
  ANY admissible choice yields the kernel's pattern.
  licence=free    P(two-sister pattern found) = 0.1081   P(+ pa in ra position 4) = 0.00094
  licence=class   P(two-sister pattern found) = 0.0029   P(+ pa in ra position 4) = 0.00002
  (TRIALS=200000, seed 20261002)

  SENSITIVITY: 'the expected internal position' for pa is position 4 only
  under one chosen segmentation of Ankhesen-pa-amun (anch-e-sen-pa-AMUN).
  If pa is merely required to be INTERNAL (positions 2..5 of 6), the
  surviving significance degrades:
    licence=free   P(pattern + pa anywhere internal) = 0.00360  family-wise over the 6 ways of picking 2 of 4 columns = 0.0214
    licence=class  P(pattern + pa anywhere internal) = 0.00014  family-wise over the 6 ways of picking 2 of 4 columns = 0.0008

==============================================================================
K4.  THE NAME SIDE OF THE BUDGET
==============================================================================
  Akhenaten and Nefertiti had SIX daughters: Meritaten, Meketaten,
  Ankhesenpaaten, Neferneferuaten-tasherit, Neferneferure, Setepenre.
  Three of the six end in -aten; two of those three begin with 'Me-'.
  So the two facts the kernel treats as constraints (3) -- 'both short
  names begin with the same syllable' and 'both end in the same divine
  name' -- are properties of EGYPTIAN ONOMASTICS, fixed before any
  Byblos sign is looked at.  They constrain the script side by exactly
  one bit each: 'the two 4-sign columns must agree in position 1 (up to
  a declarable mirror) and in position 4'.  Position 1 agreement is NOT
  observed: the two onsets are different codepoints (E49A vs E4B0) and
  are reconciled by an asserted mirror relation.  The only unpurchased
  observation left is the identity of position 4 -- one coincidence of
  p = 0.0131 per trial, against the budget computed in K1.
