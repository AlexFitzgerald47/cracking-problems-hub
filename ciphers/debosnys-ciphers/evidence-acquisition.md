**Fingerprint of the poem, for freezing before any sweep.** It comes from the poem itself, so
it is a *search key*, not a held-out test. Price its false-positive rate with a null over the
same corpus windows before believing a hit.

- 20 lines of rhymed couplets.
- Couplet 1's rhyme returns at couplet 9.
- End glyphs identical in 8 of 10 couplets (scan-verified). Couplets 5 and 7 differ only by a
  small mark.
- If a glyph is a syllable, that means an identical final syllable in at least 8 couplets.
  This is a strong filter: cyphersolver reports 31% for Moore's couplets and 61% for
  Boileau's.
- About 14 glyphs per line (range 11–17).
- Line-final punctuation `, · - - , . , , , . , . · · · · · . · ·` (· = none). Checked against
  the transcription this session.
